Our Programmes

Our programmes are designed around the development of teenagers aged approximately 13–19.

Personal Development

Self-awareness, confidence, goal setting, communication and practical life skills.

Leadership Development

Teamwork, decision-making, communication, responsibility and leadership.

Financial Literacy

Budgeting, saving, responsible spending and introductory financial planning.

Teen Entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurial mindset, opportunity identification, branding, budgeting, marketing and pitching.

Community Initiatives

Youth-focused initiatives that connect personal development with community support.

Akhona Teens Leadership Foundation

Empowering teenagers through skills, confidence and opportunities to lead and create change.
