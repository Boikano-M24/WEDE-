A complete overview of every page on the Akhona Teens website.

Site structure

Every page is one click from the home page and shares the same navigation menu and footer. Supporting files for search engines are sitemap.xml and robots.txt.

What is on each page

index.html

Hero banner, introduction, impact counters, upcoming events (loaded dynamically) and calls to action.

about.html

Our story, timeline, mission/vision/values tabs and team profiles with pop-up bios.

services.html

Searchable, filterable and sortable list of programmes and community initiatives.

events.html

Upcoming and past events with search, filters, sorting and add-to-calendar.

gallery.html

Photo gallery with category filters and a lightbox viewer.

enquiry.html

Adaptive enquiry form (enrol, volunteer, mentor, sponsor, partner) with instant response, plus FAQ accordion.

contact.html

Contact details, interactive Leaflet map with two locations and a validated message form that compiles an email.
