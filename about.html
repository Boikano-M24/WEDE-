About Us

Our story, our mission, our team

Turning personal loss into a foundation that helps teenagers lead.

Our story

Akhona Teens Leadership Foundation is a South African non-profit organisation founded in 2019 by Ruth Mthabine and headquartered in Kempton Park, Gauteng. The Foundation was established in memory of Ruth's son, Akhona, whose passing in 2017 inspired her to create a safe space where teenagers could receive the guidance, skills and support she wished had been available to her own child.

What began as a small mentorship circle for a handful of teenagers has grown into a structured leadership programme that now reaches young people across Gauteng and Mpumalanga, working alongside schools, community organisations and corporate partners. Our work is grounded in Christian principles and a simple belief: every teenager deserves to be seen, supported and equipped to lead.

Founder Ruth Mthabine with the Akhona Teens team, mentors and programme participants.

What guides us

Our mission

To equip teenagers with the personal, leadership, financial and entrepreneurial skills they need to build meaningful, self-directed futures.

Our vision

A future where every young person has the knowledge, confidence, opportunities and support needed to reach their full potential and contribute positively to their community.

Our values

  • Dignity: every teenager is valued, whatever their background.
  • Resilience: we help young people grow through challenges.
  • Integrity: we do what we say, openly and honestly.
  • Service: leadership means serving others.
  • Growth: we keep learning alongside the teenagers we serve.

Our approach

Programmes blend workshops, one-on-one mentoring and peer support groups, with a strong emphasis on practical skills teenagers can apply immediately. All volunteers and mentors are screened and trained, and all activities follow our child-safeguarding policy.

Our journey

  1. 2017

    Akhona's passing inspires Ruth Mthabine to dream of a safe space where teenagers receive the guidance and support every young person deserves.

  2. 2019

    Akhona Teens Leadership Foundation is founded in Kempton Park with a small mentorship circle.

  3. 2021

    The first A Thousand Ways to Smile dignity drive delivers packs to girls in partner schools.

  4. 2023

    The Hluvukani Innovation Hub opens in Mpumalanga, giving learners computers and free internet.

  5. 2024

    The Entrepreneurial Academy launches, ending with its first teen business showcase.

  6. 2026

    More than 3,200 teenagers reached and 4,600 girls supported through dignity packs.

Where we work

The Foundation is headquartered in Kempton Park, Gauteng, and runs regular programmes in and around Ekurhuleni. Through partnerships with schools and community organisations we also reach underserved and rural communities in Mpumalanga, including Hluvukani, where access to youth-development resources is limited.
