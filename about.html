About Akhona Teens

Akhona Teens Leadership Foundation is a youth-development organisation focused on helping teenagers build skills, confidence and leadership capacity.

Our Story

Public reporting identifies Ruth Mthabine as the founder and connects the organisation’s name to the legacy of her son, Akhona. The organisation was established to create opportunities and support for young people.

Mission

To equip teenagers with personal, leadership, financial and entrepreneurial skills that can help them build meaningful futures.

Vision

A future where young people have the knowledge, confidence, opportunities and support needed to reach their potential.

Founder

Ruth Mthabine

Founder of Akhona Teens Leadership Foundation.

Our Approach

Our work centres on personal development, leadership, financial literacy, entrepreneurship and community opportunity.
