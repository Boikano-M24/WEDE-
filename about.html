Our Story, Our Team

Turning personal loss into a foundation that helps teenagers lead.

Our Story

Akhona Teens Leadership Foundation is a South African non-profit organisation founded in 2019 by Ruth Mthabine, and is headquartered in Kempton Park, Gauteng. The Foundation was established in memory of Ruth's son, Akhona, whose passing in 2017 inspired her to create a safe space where teenagers could be equipped with the guidance, skills and support she wished had been available to her own child.

What began as a small mentorship circle for a handful of teenagers in Kempton Park has grown into a structured leadership programme that now reaches young people across Gauteng and Mpumalanga, working alongside schools, community organisations and corporate partners.

Mission

To equip teenagers with the personal, leadership, financial and entrepreneurial skills they need to build meaningful, self-directed futures.

Vision

A future where every young person has the knowledge, confidence, opportunities and support needed to reach their full potential and contribute positively to their community.

Our Team

The People Behind Akhona Teens

Founder Ruth Mthabine with the Akhona Teens team, mentors and programme participants.

Ruth Mthabine

Founder & Executive Director

Ruth founded Akhona Teens Leadership Foundation to turn personal loss into purpose. A qualified life coach and community development practitioner, she leads the organisation's programme design and oversees its partnerships with schools and funders.

Thabo Nkosi

Programmes Manager

Thabo coordinates the day-to-day running of the Personal Development, Leadership Development and Entrepreneurship programmes, and manages relationships with partner schools.

Nomvula Dlamini

Volunteer & Mentor Coordinator

Nomvula recruits, screens and supports the network of volunteers and mentors who work directly with teenagers in our programmes.

Values and Approach

Our work is grounded in Christian principles and centres on five values: dignity, resilience, integrity, service and growth. Programmes are delivered through a blend of workshops, one-on-one mentoring and peer support groups, with a strong emphasis on practical skills that teenagers can apply immediately.

Where We Work

The Foundation is headquartered in Kempton Park, Gauteng, and runs regular programmes in and around Ekurhuleni. Through partnerships with schools and community organisations, Akhona Teens also extends its reach into underserved and rural communities in Mpumalanga, including the Hluvukani area, where access to youth-development resources is limited.
