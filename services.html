Services

Our Programmes & Initiatives

Four core programme areas, plus community campaigns, all delivered through hands-on workshops and mentorship.

Core Programmes

Personal Development

Workshops and coaching aimed at helping teenagers build confidence, self-awareness and life skills for navigating personal, school and social challenges.

Leadership Development

Activities designed to help young people grow as leaders, build teamwork skills, and prepare to create positive change in their schools and communities.

Financial Literacy

Practical sessions that introduce young people to budgeting, saving and smarter financial decision-making, giving them a foundation for managing money responsibly.

Teen Entrepreneurship

The Akhona Teens Entrepreneurial Academy is a six-week practical course that walks teenagers through idea generation, budgeting, marketing and pitching, ending with a graduation showcase where participants present their own small-business ideas to local business mentors.

Community Initiatives

Beyond the Classroom

A Thousand Ways to Smile

A dignity-pack drive providing sanitary products, toiletries and confidence-building workshops to girls in underserved rural communities, with a goal of reaching 10,000 girls across Gauteng and Mpumalanga.

Hluvukani Innovation Hub

Based in the Hluvukani area of Mpumalanga, the Innovation Hub gives young people access to computers, internet connectivity and a creative space to develop ideas, complete schoolwork and take part in digital-skills workshops after school hours.

Community Outreach Support

Beyond our teen-focused programmes, our volunteers regularly support broader community outreach efforts, including visits to local schools and early-learning centres in the areas where we work.

Programme Schedule

Programmes run in school terms, with workshops typically held on weekday afternoons and Saturday sessions in Kempton Park, Ekurhuleni and Hluvukani. Participation is free, and interested teenagers or schools can register through our Enquiry page.
