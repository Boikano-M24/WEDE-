Welcome

Equipping teenagers to lead their own futures

Akhona Teens Leadership Foundation is a South African non-profit organisation that equips teenagers with the personal, leadership, financial and entrepreneurial skills they need to build meaningful futures. Founded in 2019 by Ruth Mthabine, we work with young people aged 13–19 across Gauteng and Mpumalanga.

Through hands-on workshops, mentorship and community campaigns, we help teenagers grow in confidence, discover their leadership potential, and gain practical skills for life after school.
