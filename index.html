Akhona Teens Leadership Foundation

Teen leadership programmes in Gauteng and Mpumalanga

We equip teenagers aged 13–19 with the personal, leadership, financial and entrepreneurial skills they need to build meaningful futures. Every programme is free.

Founded in 2019 by Ruth Mthabine, Akhona Teens is a South African non-profit youth-development organisation based in Kempton Park. Through workshops, one-on-one mentoring and community campaigns, we help young people grow in confidence and lead change in their own communities.

Featured campaign

A Thousand Ways to Smile

Our flagship dignity drive provides sanitary products, toiletries and confidence-building workshops to girls in underserved rural communities. The goal: reach 10,000 girls. So far more than 4,600 girls have received a dignity pack.
